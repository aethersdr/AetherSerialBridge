What the bridge does                    
Each port joins one serial device to one
TCP port. AetherSDR connects to the TCP 
port and talks to the device as if it   
were plugged into its own machine. One  
client at a time: a new connection      
replaces the current one. The device is 
opened only while a client is connected.
                                        
Read-only or administrator              
Anyone can look. To change anything, run
sudo aether-serial-bridge --tui.        
You can change ports now.
